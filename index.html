                How to use this site

                Keep this tab open — wait times update on their own. Pins and house cards use the same colours.

                House states

                Match the map circle and the card before you queue. Staff set these live.

                T18A Walk-in Aerohub Crypt Walk in No queue Just updated

                Walk-in

                There’s no queue and the house is open. Go now. Tap Alert when walk-in on other houses if you want a ping when they hit this.

                EW22 Open Dover Gate Ghouls 8 min wait Updated 2 min ago

                Open

                The house is running and taking guests. The number is the wait staff last published. Queue as normal.

                T12 Busy Atrium Abyss 32 min wait Updated 4 min ago

                Busy

                Wait is 30 minutes or more. Still taking guests, but the line is long. Check the minutes before you commit, or pick a shorter house.

                WSA Closed West Coast Warehouse Closed Not running Updated 10 min ago

                Closed

                Wait is 60 minutes or more — treated as closed (reset, last entry, or a pause). Pins and cards go red. Don’t wait at the door until it shows Open, Busy, or Walk-in again.

                Using the rest of the site

                1. Use Event Map to tap pins or house cards. Times are Singapore time, published by staff.
                2. Sign in with your admin number and name to open your stamp card. Staff stamp you at booths — you cannot stamp yourself.
                3. Six stamps unlock Fast Pass. Show it at the Fast Pass desk; staff mark it used on their side.
                4. Tonight is the programme. Games are optional. Send Feedback if a house or booth needs a note.
                5. Tap Alert when walk-in on a house for a ping when there’s no queue. On iPhone, add this site to your Home Screen first.
                6. Help desk, meetup, and skip-house notes live on Safety.
