              Using the rest of the site

              1. Open Map and tap a house card. Houses are grouped by cluster, and wait times update on their own.
              2. Sign in with your admin number and name to open your stamp card. Collect each stamp at its booth.
              3. Five stamps unlock a ticket to spin the wheel. Show it at the wheel.
              4. Send Feedback if a house or a booth needs a note.

              Walk-in alerts

              1. Sign in and choose the waits you want, from 50 minutes down to 10 in steps of 10, and also at 5 minutes. Then tap Alert when walk-in on a scare house. You are also notified when staff post an announcement.
              2. Allow notifications when your phone asks.
              3. On an iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Open that icon and tap Alert when walk-in again. A Safari tab cannot show phone alerts.
              4. Tap Stop walk-in alert on that house to turn the alert off.
