                  Stay safe tonight

                  General notes for SP Scream Fest. Follow staff instructions on the ground.

                  • Stay with your group and agree a meetup spot if you get separated.
                  • Watch your step in fog, cables, and low light. No running in queues.
                  • Drink water and take a break if you feel unwell. Tell a friend or staff.
                  • Keep bags closed and phones secure. Do not leave belongings unattended.
                  • Respect house capacity and closed areas. Staff calls are final.
                  • If someone is hurt or lost, find a facilitator or the help desk rather than crowding in.
                  • Look after each other. If a scare is too much, step out — you can skip a house.
